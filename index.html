私たちと、その先へ

to us and beyond — a poem in indigo, from first light to last

scroll slowly

暁 · first light

To keep pace is to perform — where

surrender can feel like failing beneath

the naked, unsparing glare of day,

meant only to be looked at.


But I would gladly cast a shadow over that

just so a cool breath of relief might drift through, untouched by the heat of being seen.

昼 · noon

Even the sturdiest tree will crumble

when the very earth beneath its roots is shaken by storms.,


Even the brightest blossoms must fade.

Everything learns humility — or is forced to bend —

even the quiet mountains, ancient and stronger than the fragile life that clings to them.

Yet I am a tender witness to how softly I break, and how helpless I become, the moment it concerns you.

夕 · dusk

‎It is always easier to let go than to keep tending a fire that only leaves your hands tired.

But with you, the labor of loving becomes a quiet harmony.

Nothing asks for perfection here — only truth. Simplicity becomes my way of turning heavy days into something tender and familiar —

so long as it is measured against your worth.

宵 · twilight

Nothing worth keeping was ever effortless. I love the quiet work of holding us together. ‎

‎I breathe you in,

and I will always reach for you across the dark.

夜 · midnight

Shadows make the weight of things so much clearer; the heavy mists no longer have to hide.

Your love has always been my open door — a quiet place to rest whatever cannot bear the light of day.

終 · the end

So here is what the long blue day has made:

a small bouquet, tied with a strip of selvedge,

for you who stayed to watch the light go out.

藍の花束

a bouquet for the end of the light
